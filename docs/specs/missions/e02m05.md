## 5. Flow summary
1. Arrival from e02m03. Xavier joins the party. On the first fade-in, "EnterSubbase" and then "…" play. **Objective 7 is completed** and **objective 2 "…"** is added. Choosing the tour sets fd8=1.
2. While touring (fd8=1), the leader triggers one-shot Xavier lines: Medical, Brig, BeastLab, Hangar, Controlroom (fade-back after each), plus Transport and Cerebro. Talking to Xavier ("…") can end the tour (fd8=0). Beast can be chatted with.
3. **Win trigger:** entering `warRoom` (touring leader: after "MeetWarroom"; otherwise right away). Pressing action on a `toFloor1x` elevator does the same. Ending sequence: Xavier leaves the party, fade, "NextMission" (objective 2 completed silently), party teleported into the War Room with Wolverine, Cyclops, Xavier and Beast, "NextMission2" HAARP briefing, fade.
4. **CompleteEpisode(1)**: episode idx 2 → 3 (`e03m06`, spawn1; team select is free choice, locked mask 0x04A). No SetNextMovie. No hero unlocks in this script.

## 5. Flow summary
1. Arrival (episode idx 2). After the fade-in, **Phoenix (5), Storm (9) and Beast (0) are unlocked**. Jean plays "…", then **objective 4 "…"** is added.
2. Optional: room zones play one-shot Jean lines. Students, bookcases and containers are flavour. Containers give at most 10 random consumables.
3. When the leader walks up to Xavier (ff8=1), a cinematic starts: objective 4 is completed and **objective 7 "…"** is added. The party is teleported into the office, then "MeetProfX" plays (first visit) or "ProfX-1" (later visits).
   - Result 1 (Jean keeps showing her around): control comes back, and the cinematic re-arms after 10 s.
   - Result 0 (subbasement): fade, then **ChangeLevel("e02m05", 1)**.
4. Other exits: `toLevel2x`/`toSubBase` → ChangeLevel("e02m04", 1) (2nd floor). `toOutside` (only after MeetBackdoor for the leader) → ChangeLevel("e02m11", 1).
5. This mission has no win condition and no CompleteEpisode. Progress continues in e02m05.
